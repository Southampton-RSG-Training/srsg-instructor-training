Just as in our technical workshops, we collect feedback at the end of
Instructor Training. This will help us continually improve this training.

## One Up, One Down

10 mins.

Provide one up, one down feedback on the entire Instructor Training course. Remember:

- Say only one thing, and try not to duplicate. This gets harder for
  those who come later!
- Trainers should try not to respond, only record responses (e.g. in the
  shared document). This is also hard, but important!
